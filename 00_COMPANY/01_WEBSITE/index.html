About Us

A studio built for the next round

03

VELQUIA makes casual games that feel clear in seconds and surprising for months. We care about the mechanic, the light, and the reason someone opens the game again tomorrow.

We design in small teams: one idea, a tight loop, and art that belongs to that loop. When a game ships, we stay with it — new seasons, cleaner feel, and updates that respect the people already playing.

01

The first tap teaches the game. No manual required.

02

Surprising for months

03

Alive after launch

Seasons and balance passes that give players a reason to return.

01 — Game Development

Games

VELQUIA's core business. We plan and develop games, then distribute and sell them digitally.

  • Puzzle, PC, and smartphone games
  • Original game planning and development
  • Worlds and characters inside our games

02 — Digital Talent & Music

Digital Talent & Music

We connect characters and fans through VTubers and music creators.

  • VTuber planning, operations, and management
  • Vocaloid tracks and original songs
  • Streams, videos, background music, and other music content

03 — Digital Publishing & Media

Publishing & Media

We carry our stories and worlds further through fiction, video, and the web.

  • Light novels and web novels, from planning to digital publishing
  • Character art, illustration, and video
  • Web media, news, and promotion for our works

04 — App Development

AI / SaaS Apps

Subscription products that automate content, support, and operations for creators and local businesses.

  • Each game runs on its own local server (no shared control panel)
  • Standalone apps and studio tools live in their own folders
  • Relixia project files stay untouched
